# Original portfolio resources

The 3D room, models, baked textures, shaders, monitor overlays, videos, sound
effects, camera controls, and interface controls are retained from Henry
Heffernan's portfolio. The original copyright and permission notice is retained
in `LICENSE.md`.

The desktop window, button, drag and resize indicators, icon registry, icon
images, fonts, colors, and base stylesheet in `src/Desktop/` are adapted from
https://github.com/henryjeff/portfolio-inner-site at revision `23cf84a`.
The desktop application, invitation navigation, taskbar and graduation content
are adapted for this project. Upstream design and asset credits remain visible
in the desktop's Credits section.

Original asset credits:

- Mickael Boitte: computer model.

Event facts, invitation copy, RSVP RPC client, invitation manager, dashboard,
photographs and signature are copied/adapted from the owner's local `gradar`
project. Source Serif 4 is distributed under the included `SourceSerif4-OFL.md`.
